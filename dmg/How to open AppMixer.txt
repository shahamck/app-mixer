AppMixer — volume for every app, right in your menu bar
=====================================================

Install
1. Drag AppMixer into the Applications folder.
2. Open Applications and double-click AppMixer.

First launch: "AppMixer can't be opened" / "Apple could not verify…"
AppMixer isn't from the Mac App Store, so macOS asks you to confirm once:
1. Click Done (or OK) on that message.
2. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security.
3. Scroll down to the message about AppMixer and click "Open Anyway".
4. Enter your password, then click Open.
You only need to do this the first time.

Using it
• Click the slider icon in the menu bar.
• Quiet: just your output device and volume.
• When apps play sound, each one appears under the speaker with its own volume.
• When an app uses the microphone, the mic level appears too.
• Click the device name to switch speakers or microphones.
• Click either speaker icon to mute.
• AirPods and other Bluetooth devices show their battery (left, right, case).
• Right-click the panel for options and Quit.

The first time you change an app's volume, macOS asks to let AppMixer capture
audio from other apps. Allow it; nothing is recorded or saved.

Requires macOS 14.2 or later. Works on Apple silicon and Intel Macs.
